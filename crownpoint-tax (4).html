FAQ

Common questions.

What is an Enrolled Agent? +

An EA is the highest credential the IRS awards. We're federally licensed to represent any taxpayer in any IRS matter — audits, notices, appeals — in all 50 states.

How much does it cost? +

Monthly advisory packages start at $299/mo. Individual returns start at $250. Business returns start at $600. All flat-fee — no surprises.

I got an IRS notice. Can you help? +

Yes. As an EA, Crown Point handles all IRS and FTB matters directly — audits, CP2000 notices, back taxes, and appeals. Don't respond alone. Call 714-745-3060.

Do you work outside California? +

Yes. We serve clients in all 50 states with no geographic restrictions, fully remote via phone, email, and secure document sharing.
